### About me

Hi, I'm exusxt. I like taking tools that people actually need and turning them into polished, cross-platform desktop apps. Everything I publish is free, open source and MIT licensed — no accounts, no telemetry, no strings attached.

When I'm not writing TypeScript and C#, I'm usually tinkering with retro hardware. The N64 is my favorite console, and a good chunk of my repos orbit the SummerCart64 flash cart: menus, ROM managers, SD card builders and save tools.
